## フル記事（Medium/Substack・長文X 用・約330語）

**Marx v Savea: the star-studded final you're probably missing**

This Sunday, Japan's top flight crowns its champion — and the team sheets read like a Rugby Championship squad list.

In the League One final at Tokyo's National Stadium, **Kobelco Kobe Steelers** meet **Kubota Spears**, two sides stacked with international talent.

Kobe are built around All Blacks superstar **Ardie Savea**, New Zealand's reigning Player of the Year, alongside lock **Brodie Retallick** and midfielder **Anton Lienert-Brown** — all under former Australia head coach Dave Rennie.

Kubota answer with Springbok hooker **Malcolm Marx**, the reigning World Rugby Player of the Year, ex-Chiefs fullback **Shaun Stevenson**, and former Wallabies playmaker **Bernard Foley**.

**The road here**
Kobe arrive in frightening form. In the semifinal they dismantled Tokyo Suntory Sungoliath 69-23, the latest in a run of high-scoring wins.

Kubota took the harder road, edging Saitama Wild Knights 26-24 in their semifinal after a 26-3 quarterfinal win over Toshiba Brave Lupus. Where Kobe overwhelm teams, Kubota grind them down.

**The sub-plot**
The two already met in the regular season, on 10 May — and Kobe won it by five, 24-19. Kubota will relish the rematch on the biggest stage.

**Why it's worth your time**
For English-speaking fans, Japanese club rugby is criminally under-covered. Yet here is a final pitting the reigning World Player of the Year against New Zealand's Player of the Year, backed by a supporting cast of current and recent All Blacks and Springboks. If you love Test rugby, this is Test-quality talent — in a competition you might not be watching yet.
